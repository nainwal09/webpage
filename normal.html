Our Design

A cutting-edge approach to fish tank management

Innovative Technology Meets Aquatic Care

Our automated fish tank system uses dual ESP32 microcontrollers and an ESP32 CAM to create a seamless, intelligent aquarium management system. The design incorporates:

  • Computer vision for fish monitoring
  • IoT-controlled feeding mechanisms
  • Real-time pH monitoring
  • Smart water filtration system
  • Mobile app integration

Designed with both functionality and aesthetics in mind, our system ensures your aquatic friends receive optimal care while providing you with an elegant display piece.

Why AI in Fish Tanks?

Transforming aquatic care through artificial intelligence

Smart Monitoring

Our teachable machine AI model constantly observes fish behavior to detect unusual patterns that might indicate health issues.

Adaptive Feeding

AI algorithms learn your fish's eating patterns and automatically adjust feeding schedules and quantities for optimal nutrition.

Water Quality Prediction

Our predictive models analyze water parameter trends to forecast potential issues before they become critical.

Computer Vision Technology

Our ESP32-CAM implementation uses TensorFlow Lite to power an advanced fish monitoring system that:

  • Identifies different fish species in your tank
  • Monitors fish health through behavior analysis
  • Detects and alerts you to potential aggression between fish
  • Tracks growth patterns over time
  • Provides visual feeding confirmation

The system continuously learns and improves its accuracy through regular updates and your feedback.

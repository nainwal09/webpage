Why AI in Aquariums

Smart Monitoring

Real-time data analysis

Intelligent Monitoring

Our AI systems constantly analyze water parameters, temperature, and fish behavior to detect potential issues before they become problems.

Benefits include:

  • 24/7 monitoring without human intervention
  • Early detection of parameter fluctuations
  • Automated alerts when issues are detected

Precision Feeding

Optimized nutrition delivery

AI-Powered Feeding

Our system uses computer vision and teachable machine AI models to determine when your fish are hungry, automatically dispensing the perfect amount of food based on species-specific needs.

Features include:

  • Custom feeding schedules based on fish type
  • Manual override with timer functionality
  • Food quantity optimization to reduce waste

Water Quality

Automated water management

Intelligent Water Management

Our pH sensors and water analysis technology continuously monitor water quality, automatically adjusting filtration and treatment systems.

Key benefits:

  • Real-time pH level monitoring
  • Automated water change reminders
  • Smart submersible pumps for optimal filtration

Powerful microcontroller backbone

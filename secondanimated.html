Why AI in Aquariums?

🐟

Smart Fish Recognition

Intelligent monitoring of fish behavior and health

Smart Fish Recognition

Our AI system uses computer vision through ESP32-CAM to identify different fish species, monitor their swimming patterns, and detect unusual behavior that might indicate health problems. The system learns your specific fish over time, providing personalized care recommendations based on species, age, and behavior patterns.

🍽️

Optimized Feeding

Precise, automated feeding based on real needs

Optimized Feeding

Using Teachable Machine AI models, our system optimizes feeding schedules and portions based on fish activity, species requirements, and tank conditions. It prevents overfeeding (a common cause of water quality issues) and ensures all fish receive proper nutrition. The system can identify when certain fish aren't getting enough food and adjust dispensing patterns accordingly.

💧

Water Quality Prediction

Proactive maintenance for perfect water conditions

Water Quality Prediction

Our AI doesn't just monitor current water parameters—it predicts future changes based on historical data, bioload, feeding patterns, and even seasonal factors. The system can forecast pH fluctuations, ammonia spikes, and other water quality issues days before they become problematic, allowing for preventive maintenance rather than reactive corrections, which significantly reduces fish stress and mortality.

Smart Features

📷

AI Vision Monitoring

ESP32-CAM for real-time observation

AI Vision Monitoring

Our system uses ESP32-CAM to continuously monitor your aquarium, providing real-time video feeds and image analysis. The camera tracks fish movement, detects potential health issues, and even counts fish to ensure none are missing. The integrated computer vision algorithms can identify aggressive behavior, signs of disease, and breeding activity.

🤖

Smart Feeding System

Teachable Machine AI for perfect portions

Smart Feeding System

Using Teachable Machine AI models, our automated feeding system learns your fish's eating patterns and adjusts accordingly. The precision feeder can dispense exact amounts based on species requirements, preventing waste and improving water quality. Manual override and scheduling options available through the app give you complete control.

🧪

Water Quality Control

pH sensors with automated filtration

Water Quality Control

Our integrated pH level sensors continuously monitor water quality, while the submersible pump automatically filters and refreshes water as needed. The system tracks multiple parameters including ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and temperature, providing alerts before problems occur and automatically adjusting filtration when needed.

⏱️

Scheduled Maintenance

Automated timers with manual override

Scheduled Maintenance

Set up custom maintenance schedules for feeding, water changes, and filter cleaning. Our smart timers ensure perfect timing every time, but you can easily switch to manual mode when you want more control. The manual feeding timer allows you to schedule precise feeding times regardless of whether you're home or away.

📱

Mobile Control

Full control from anywhere in the world

Mobile Control

Control every aspect of your aquarium from our intuitive mobile app. Watch live video feeds, adjust lighting, trigger feeding, or monitor water parameters from anywhere. The app sends critical alerts about water quality, fish health, and maintenance needs directly to your smartphone.

💡

Adaptive Lighting

Smart LED system that mimics natural cycles

Adaptive Lighting

Our programmable LED lighting system replicates natural day/night cycles and can simulate specific underwater environments. Perfect for encouraging natural behavior in fish and optimal growth in aquatic plants. The lights automatically adjust based on the season, fish species, and plant requirements.
